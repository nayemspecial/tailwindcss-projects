Platform Overview

EduTech Pro is a comprehensive Learning Management System designed for universities, corporate training, and online education providers. The platform transforms traditional learning into engaging digital experiences with interactive features.

We built a scalable solution supporting video streaming, live classes, interactive quizzes, progress tracking, and certification generation. The platform now serves 50+ educational institutions with 25,000+ active students.

Development Process

How we built EduTech Pro from concept to launch

Phase 1: Educational Research

Weeks 1-3
Research

Conducted extensive research with educators, students, and institutions. Analyzed learning methodologies, curriculum structures, and pedagogical approaches.

Phase 2: Architecture Design

Weeks 4-6
Design

Designed scalable architecture for video streaming, course management, and assessment systems. Created wireframes and interactive prototypes.

Phase 3: Core Development

Weeks 7-14
Development

Developed Laravel backend with video streaming API, course management system, and assessment engine. Built React.js frontend with real-time features.

Phase 4: Video & Assessment

Weeks 15-18
Development

Implemented WebRTC for live classes, HLS for video streaming, and advanced assessment system with AI-powered proctoring and plagiarism detection.

Phase 5: Testing & Launch

Weeks 19-20
Launch

Conducted comprehensive testing with real students and educators. Deployed to AWS with CDN for global video delivery and launched with 5 pilot institutions.

Challenges & Solutions

How we overcame technical and educational challenges

Challenge: Scalable Video Streaming

Supporting 10,000+ concurrent video streams with adaptive quality for different bandwidths and devices.

Challenge: Secure Assessments

Preventing cheating in online exams while maintaining accessibility and user-friendly experience.

Challenge: Data Privacy Compliance

Ensuring GDPR, COPPA, and other educational data privacy regulations compliance for global institutions.

Solution: CDN + Adaptive Streaming

Implemented CloudFront CDN with HLS adaptive streaming, automatic quality adjustment, and regional edge caching.

Solution: AI Proctoring System

Developed AI-powered proctoring with face detection, screen monitoring, and behavior analysis for secure assessments.

Solution: Privacy-First Architecture

Built data anonymization, consent management, and regional data storage with automatic compliance reporting.
