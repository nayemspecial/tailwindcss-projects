Project Overview

StockMaster is an enterprise-grade inventory management system built for a leading retail chain with 50+ locations. The client needed a unified solution to manage inventory across all stores, warehouses, and distribution centers in real-time.

We developed a monolithic Laravel application with barcode/RFID integration, automated reordering, and comprehensive reporting. The system now manages over 50,000 SKUs with 99.5% accuracy and reduced manual labor by 60%.

Key Objectives

  • Barcode/RFID scanning for accurate data entry

Core Features

Advanced inventory management capabilities

Barcode/RFID Integration

Scan barcodes and RFID tags for instant inventory updates. Supports QR codes, UPC, EAN, and custom barcode formats with mobile scanning capabilities.

Real-time Inventory Tracking

Live inventory updates across all locations. Track stock movements, transfers, and adjustments in real-time with audit trails for all transactions.

Supply Chain Management

Manage vendors, purchase orders, and shipments. Automated reordering based on stock levels, lead times, and sales forecasts.

Advanced Reporting & Analytics

Generate comprehensive reports: stock levels, turnover rates, sales trends, and predictive analytics for demand forecasting.

Multi-location Management

Manage inventory across warehouses, stores, and distribution centers. Transfer stock between locations with full tracking.

Mobile Inventory App

Native mobile apps for iOS and Android with offline capabilities, barcode scanning, and real-time sync when online.

How we built StockMaster from requirements to deployment

Challenges & Solutions

Technical hurdles and innovative solutions

Challenge: Multi-location Real-time Sync

Synchronizing inventory data across 50+ locations with varying internet connectivity while maintaining data consistency and preventing conflicts.

Challenge: Hardware Compatibility

Supporting multiple barcode scanner brands, RFID readers, and label printers from different manufacturers with varying communication protocols.

Challenge: Performance at Scale

Handling 50,000+ SKUs with real-time updates, complex reporting, and maintaining sub-second response times during peak inventory operations.

Solution: Optimistic Sync Architecture

Implemented conflict resolution algorithms, offline capability with local database, and intelligent sync queuing for reliable multi-location operations.

Solution: Hardware Abstraction Layer

Created a unified hardware API that supports multiple device types, with automatic detection and configuration for different scanner models.

Solution: Advanced Caching & Indexing

Implemented Redis caching for frequently accessed data, database query optimization, and materialized views for complex reports.
